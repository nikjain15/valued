Six gates

VValue

  • Studied one advocate across a whole shift: 12 stages
  • 40 to 60 calls a shift, members 65 and older
  • Moves handle time, note completeness, repeat contacts

AAI-fit

  • Advocate-side first, not a member chat
  • Rules as a graph: the path is the citation
  • Three verdicts by construction, checked by a gate

LLabels

  • Labelled by intent, independent of the rules
  • English 143, Spanish 31, interpreter 21
  • An adjudicated subset, simulated in the prototype

UUser-in-the-loop

  • Never speaks to the member
  • Never crosses: coverage, prior auth, length of stay, clinical
  • Anything no rule covers goes to a person

EEvals

  • Every gate by slice, in CI
  • Stand-in judge refused for answer correctness at 0.84
  • Escalation is never given to a judge

DDeploy and drive

  • Designed: shadow, then 5% canary for 3 days
  • Designed: kill switch per skill, named owner
  • Designed: nothing changes itself, two people sign

Decisions, and the alternative not taken

  • Advocate-side first over a member chat. A person stays between the model and the member.
  • Rules first over a model call per turn. The eval is exact and the stop is provable.
  • Rules as a graph over vector search. A chunk returns the ER copay and stops.
  • The advocate ticks identity over auto-verify. Identity is a human decision here.

Risks and how they are held

  • Coverage advice: held by type, order and the person, independently
  • A language failing silently: held by labelling every language and gating by slice
  • A judge drifting: held by validation against labels, refused below the bar
